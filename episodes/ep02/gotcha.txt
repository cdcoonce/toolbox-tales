Every working session starts with no memory of the last one. One session finished real work late in the day: a fix merged, a decision made, a dead end ruled out. Nobody wrote down where things stood. The next morning's session started blank, saw the old problem, rebuilt infrastructure that already existed, re-proposed the plan that had been rejected the night before, and walked straight back into the dead end. A whole day went into redoing yesterday. The work was never the problem; the hand-over was.
